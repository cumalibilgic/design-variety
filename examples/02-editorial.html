Every box begins at a farm we have visited. We name the producer, the altitude, the harvest month, and what we paid. Nothing is blended to hide a weak lot, and nothing sits on a shelf longer than ten days.

The result is a cup that tastes like the place it came from: blueberry and jasmine from the Guji highlands, red apple and caramel from Huila. We write the tasting notes by hand, and we are usually arguing about them.
